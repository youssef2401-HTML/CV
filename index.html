About Me

I look at school as something more than just classrooms and exams. For me, it's a space to build things, lead initiatives, and learn how people tick. I love taking ideas off the paper and making them happen in the real world—whether that's through debugging a clean line of code or coordinating a school event.

My background splits right down the middle between strict logic and creative expression. I am constantly trying to acquire new skills, step up for new responsibilities, and find better ways to organize projects.
